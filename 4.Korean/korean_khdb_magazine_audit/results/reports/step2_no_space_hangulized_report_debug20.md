## Cleaning Rules

- Unicode NFKC normalization.
- Remove KHDB page markers such as `<44>`, `<24-42>`, and `<256-257>`.
- Remove all Unicode whitespace.
- Preserve Hanja, Hangul, punctuation, digits, Latin text, and symbols except page markers.
